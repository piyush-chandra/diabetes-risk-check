The numbers, honestly measured

Every algorithm below was scored the same unforgiving way: repeated 5-fold cross-validation (5 folds × 4 repeats) — every record is predicted only by models that never trained on it — and 95% confidence intervals from 2,000 bootstrap resamples. No accuracy alone, no single lucky split.

Cross-validated performance of 10 algorithms and a stacking ensemble on the diabetes dataset

↓ Brier is a loss: lower is better. Sensitivity = recall on positive cases — in screening this matters most, because a missed case is the expensive error.

How to read this

ROC-AUC measures ranking quality across all thresholds (1.0 = perfect). Brier rewards being well-calibrated — saying 70% only when it's truly 70%. The deployed model is the stacking ensemble: it trades a sliver of raw accuracy for the best calibration, because the tool reports honest probabilities, not just labels.
